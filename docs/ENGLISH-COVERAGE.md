## Verification

- Content validation: unique IDs, exact grammar/pattern allocations, all level/category combinations, short explanations, bilingual examples, verb forms and tense labels, valid quiz choices, static route coverage.
- Browser checks: every lesson renders, quiz feedback and review, saved drafts/answers/completion, search/filter/pagination, mobile layout/navigation, Java route regression.
- Language QA: original rows are authored; expanded English definitions/examples are adapted from the CC0 TKG learner dictionary and Princeton WordNet 3.0, with their license notices retained. New Tamil translations are machine-assisted, with sample review and explicit corrections. Automated checks do not establish linguistic accuracy. See public/content/english/SOURCES.md.
- Listening: browser speech synthesis when an English voice is available; transcript fallback otherwise. There are no prerecorded audio files.

## Remaining numerical targets

The vocabulary target is complete at 4,500 unique lexical items, including words, phrasal verbs and fixed expressions. Entries have real definitions and bilingual examples; spelling variants and repeated senses are not extra items. Other skill/category quotas remain below target where shown. Completion percentages measure this catalog, not certified CEFR attainment. Level allocation is a custom learning order informed by frequency/profile evidence; it is not an official CEFR word classification.
