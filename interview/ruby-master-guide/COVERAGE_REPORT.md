## Counting and deduplication rules

Counts describe extracted question/concept occurrences, not numbers advertised by source websites. Compound prompts stay together when they share a code/scenario context; all their subparts remain in the source and reviewed explanation. Standalone technical headings are counted as concept prompts. Exact duplicates normalize case, whitespace, numbering and typographic quotes. Context-dependent/output/MCQ prompts also require matching source context. Semantic duplicates use narrowly defined equivalence rules and explicit reviewed approvals. Different comparisons and follow-ups remain separate. Unique count = occurrences − exact duplicates − semantic duplicates. Conservative non-merges are intentional when scope differs. All original occurrences map to one final ID in [QUESTION_COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv](QUESTION_COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv). Duplicate answers’ distinct reviewed explanations/examples remain under their final question.
