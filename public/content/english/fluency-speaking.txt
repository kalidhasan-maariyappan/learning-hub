=One decision I would reconsider is accepting too many tasks during a busy month. At the time, I wanted to be helpful and assumed that I could manage everything by working faster. In practice, I spent more time switching between tasks than completing them. I eventually asked a colleague for help and agreed on clearer priorities with my manager. If I had explained the workload earlier, we could have avoided some unnecessary stress. The experience taught me that being reliable does not mean saying yes to every request.
